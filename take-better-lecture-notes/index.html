How to take better lecture notes (and use them to study)

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To take better lecture notes, write the main ideas and examples in your own words instead of copying slides, leave space to add questions, and spend ten minutes after class turning your notes into quiz questions. Notes help most when you use them to test yourself, not just reread.

What should you write down?

Main ideas, examples, and anything the professor repeats or says will be on the exam. Skip what's already on the slides you can download. Putting ideas in your own words makes you process them, which is where learning starts.

What's the Cornell method?

Split the page: notes on the right, a narrow column on the left for questions, and a summary at the bottom. After class, write questions in the left column that your notes answer. Later, cover the right side and quiz yourself from the questions.

Should you type or handwrite?

Either works if you write in your own words. A 2014 study by Mueller and Oppenheimer found laptop note-takers tended to copy lectures word for word and did worse on concept questions, though later studies found smaller differences. The habit matters more than the tool.

What do you do with notes after class?

Turn them into questions within a day, then quiz yourself through the week. A set of questions from your own notes is one of the best study guides you can make.

Turning notes and slides into quizzes

Utterseen is a free AI voice assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Open your notes or the lecture slides, hold a key and say "quiz me on this" or "make a study guide," and it works from what's on your screen. It can summarize recorded lectures too.

Questions people ask

Is it better to type or handwrite notes?

Both work if you summarize in your own words. Copying word for word is the habit to avoid, and it's easier to fall into when typing fast.

What is the Cornell note-taking method?

A page split into notes, a question column and a summary. You write questions after class and use them to quiz yourself.

Can AI turn my notes into a quiz?

Yes. Give it your notes and ask for questions. Utterseen can quiz you out loud from notes or slides on your screen.

Sources
