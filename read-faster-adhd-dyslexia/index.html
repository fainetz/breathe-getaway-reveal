Reading tools that help with ADHD and dyslexia

November 18, 2026

Short answer: If long readings are hard with ADHD or dyslexia, have the text read out loud while you follow along, break it into short chunks, and ask for a plain-language explanation of anything dense. Mac and Windows both have free built-in read-aloud features, and AI tools can explain the text in simpler words.

How do you get a page read out loud for free?

On a Mac, turn on Speak selection in System Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, then select text and press Option-Esc. On Windows, the Microsoft Edge browser has Read aloud for web pages and PDFs, and Narrator reads anything on screen.

Why does listening while reading help?

Hearing the words while your eyes follow along keeps your place and takes some of the decoding work off. Many people find they get through readings faster and remember more than when reading silently.

How do you get through a long reading?

Break it into sections and set a goal per section, like "find the main point." After each one, say the main idea out loud in a sentence. Short sections with a clear goal are easier to start and easier to finish.

What about dense or confusing text?

Ask an AI to explain the paragraph in plain words, or to give you the main point first and then the details. Getting the big picture first makes the details easier to follow.

Reading help on any page

Utterseen is a free AI voice assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Hold a key and ask it to read the page out loud or explain it in plain words; answers come point-first in short sentences. It works in any app, including PDFs, web pages and course sites.

Questions people ask

Is there a free text-to-speech on Mac?

Yes. Turn on Speak selection under System Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content. Select text and press Option-Esc to hear it.

How do I make Windows read a PDF out loud?

Open the PDF in Microsoft Edge and use Read aloud. Narrator, built into Windows, can also read anything on screen.

Can AI simplify a hard reading?

Yes. Ask for the main point first, then a plain-language explanation of each paragraph. Utterseen can do this out loud for whatever is on your screen.
