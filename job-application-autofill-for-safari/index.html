Job application autofill for Safari: what actually works on a Mac

November 18, 2026

Short answer: Most job application autofill tools, including Simplify, Teal, Jobright and Huntr, are built as Chrome and Firefox extensions, so they don't run in Safari on a Mac. What works in Safari: Utterseen, a Mac app that fills applications in any browser and makes a resume tailored to each job; Safari extensions like ApplySuite and SimpleFill; and Simplify's iOS app on iPhone.

Why is Safari harder?

Safari extensions have to be built and distributed separately through Apple, so most job tools only ship Chrome and Firefox versions. Mac users often keep a second browser just for applying.

Which is the most complete?

Utterseen, because it isn't limited to one browser and does more than autofill: on a posting you say "apply to this job," it makes a resume tailored to that job, fills the form, and asks you the written questions.

What about using another browser?

That works too: some Mac users keep Chrome, Edge or Arc just for applying with an extension. A desktop app avoids the switch. More: best apps to apply to jobs faster.

Questions people ask

Does Simplify work in Safari?

Simplify's desktop extension is for Chrome-based browsers and Firefox; its iOS app supports Safari on iPhone. On Mac Safari, Utterseen works because it's a desktop app.

What is the best job autofill for Mac?

Utterseen works in Safari and any other browser on Mac and also makes a resume tailored to each job before filling the application.
