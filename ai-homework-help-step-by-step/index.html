How to get homework help from AI that actually teaches you

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To get homework help from AI that teaches you, ask it to explain one step at a time and wait for you to try the next step, instead of asking for the final answer. When you get stuck, ask for a hint or a similar worked example. That way you can solve the next problem, and the test, on your own.

What should you ask the AI?

Instead of "what's the answer," try "walk me through the first step and stop" or "give me a hint, not the answer." For essays, ask it to point out where your argument is weak rather than rewrite it. You do the thinking; the AI fills the gaps.

What if you don't understand the explanation?

Ask it to explain it a different way: simpler words, an analogy, or "like I'm in ninth grade." Ask for a similar problem with the steps shown, then try your own again. Two or three angles usually make it click.

How do you check you actually learned it?

Ask for one more problem of the same type and solve it without help. If you can, you've got it. If not, you know exactly which step to go back to.

Is it allowed?

Using AI to understand material is like using a tutor or office hours. Turning in AI-written answers as your own usually isn't allowed, and some classes limit AI use entirely, so check your teacher's policy.

A tutor that sees your homework

Utterseen is a free AI voice assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. With a worksheet, problem set or online homework open, hold a key and ask "help me with number 4"; it explains step by step out loud, like a tutor, without you typing out the problem.

Questions people ask

Can AI solve math homework?

Many AI tools can solve math problems, but they can make mistakes. Ask for the steps, follow along, and check the answer, so you learn the method and catch errors.

What's the best AI for homework help?

One that explains step by step and lets you ask follow-up questions. Utterseen does this out loud from whatever homework is on your screen.

Will teachers know I used AI?

If you only use AI to understand the material and write your own answers, there's nothing to hide. Follow your class policy on AI use.
