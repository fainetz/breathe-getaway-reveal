Simplify vs Teal vs Huntr vs Utterseen: job application tools compared

November 18, 2026

Short answer: Simplify, Teal, Huntr and Utterseen each solve a different part of applying. Simplify autofills forms from a saved profile in your browser, Teal builds resumes and matches keywords, Huntr tracks your applications, and Utterseen makes a resume tailored to each job and fills out the application by voice in any browser. Pick based on what takes you the most time.

Which is best for autofilling applications?

Simplify if you only use Chrome and want quick standard fields. Utterseen if you want it to work in any browser and also make a tailored resume for each job before filling the form.

Which is best for resumes?

Teal for building and polishing a resume from scratch. Utterseen for turning your existing resume into a version tailored to each posting in one step.

Which is best for tracking?

Huntr or Teal. Utterseen doesn't track, so pair it with one of them or a spreadsheet.

Can you use more than one?

Yes, and many people do: Utterseen to tailor and fill each application, Huntr or Teal to track everything.

Questions people ask

Is Simplify or Utterseen better?

Simplify is a quick autofill extension for supported browsers. Utterseen works in any browser and also makes a resume tailored to each job before filling the application, by voice.

Is Teal or Huntr better for tracking jobs?

Both track applications well. Teal leans toward resume building; Huntr toward a visual job board.

Which job application tool saves the most time?

The one that removes your slowest step. For most people that's filling forms and tailoring resumes, which Utterseen does together.
