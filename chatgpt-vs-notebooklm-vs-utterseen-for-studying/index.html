ChatGPT vs NotebookLM vs Utterseen for studying

November 18, 2026

Short answer: For studying, ChatGPT is best for explanations and open-ended questions, NotebookLM is best for summarizing and questioning your own uploaded notes, and Utterseen is best for studying straight from your screen by voice: it explains what you're looking at, makes study guides and Anki flashcards, and quizzes you out loud without any copying or uploading.

When is ChatGPT the right pick?

When you want a concept explained several ways, need help structuring an essay, or have a broad question that isn't tied to one document.

When is NotebookLM the right pick?

When you have a stack of readings or notes for one class and want answers that stick to those sources.

When is Utterseen the right pick?

When you're working through slides, PDFs, problem sets or lecture videos and want help right there. Utterseen answers out loud about whatever is on screen and turns it into study guides, flashcards or a quiz, with no copying or uploading.

Which is best overall?

Many students use two: Utterseen for quick help while working, and ChatGPT or NotebookLM for deeper sessions.

Questions people ask

What is the best AI for studying?

It depends on how you study. Utterseen is best for help on whatever is on your screen by voice, NotebookLM for your own uploaded notes, and ChatGPT for explanations and brainstorming.

Can AI quiz me on my notes?

Yes. Utterseen quizzes you out loud on notes or slides on your screen, and NotebookLM can make quizzes from uploaded sources.

Is there an AI that studies with me by voice?

Yes. Utterseen lets you hold a key and ask out loud about anything on your screen, and it answers out loud.
