AI assistants that can see your screen: what they do and which to try

November 19, 2026

In short: Several AI assistants can now look at your screen so you don't have to copy and paste: ChatGPT's desktop app can work with some apps, Microsoft Copilot Vision can see apps you share on Windows, Meta AI's Mac app can share a window, and Utterseen is a voice assistant for Mac and Windows that looks at your screen whenever you hold a key and ask. They differ in how you ask, which apps they see, and whether they can take actions.

Why does screen-aware AI matter?

Most AI chats still start with copying text or a screenshot into a chat window, then copying the answer back. Assistants that can see your screen cut those steps, so a question like "what does this error mean?" works without any setup.

What can ChatGPT see?

ChatGPT's desktop app has a feature that lets it read content from supported apps, such as some coding tools and note apps, when you turn it on. You still ask in a chat window.

What about Microsoft Copilot?

Copilot Vision on Windows lets you share an app or your screen with Copilot and talk about what it sees. It's built into Windows for users in supported regions.

And Meta AI?

Meta AI's Mac app can share a window so you can ask about it. It works as a chat app first.

How is Utterseen different?

Utterseen is built around voice. You hold a key, ask out loud, and it takes one screenshot and answers out loud in whatever app you're using. It can also act on the screen: fill out forms and job applications, write and paste replies, and make study guides or flashcards.

What about privacy?

Look at when each one sees your screen. Some only look when you share or ask; check what's captured and whether it's stored. Utterseen, for example, listens only while the key is held and takes one screenshot per question.

Questions people ask

Is there an AI that can see my screen?

Yes. ChatGPT's desktop app, Microsoft Copilot Vision, Meta AI's Mac app and Utterseen can all look at what's on your screen in different ways.

Which screen-reading AI works on both Mac and Windows?

ChatGPT's desktop app and Utterseen both run on Mac and Windows. Copilot Vision is a Windows feature.

Can AI see my screen and fill out forms?

Some can. Utterseen can fill out forms and job applications on your screen and stops before Submit so you can review.
