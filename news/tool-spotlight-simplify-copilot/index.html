Tool spotlight: Simplify Copilot, the free autofill extension for job applications

November 19, 2026

In short: Simplify Copilot is a free browser extension that saves your resume details once and autofills job applications on many common job sites, then tracks the jobs you've applied to. It's popular with students and early-career job seekers who apply in volume.

What does Simplify do well?

Speed on standard forms. Contact details, work history and education fill in with a click on supported sites, and every application lands in a tracker automatically.

Where does it fall short?

It's a browser extension, so it only works in supported browsers, and custom or unusual application forms can still need a lot of manual fixing. It fills from a saved profile rather than tailoring your resume to each posting.

What are the alternatives?

Teal and Jobscan focus on matching your resume to the posting, Huntr on tracking, and Utterseen makes a resume tailored to each job and fills the application by voice in any browser. See our full comparison.

Should you use it?

If you apply mostly in Chrome on standard job sites, it's an easy free win. Check every field before you submit.

Questions people ask

Is Simplify Copilot free?

Yes, the core extension is free, with optional paid upgrades.

Does Simplify work on every job site?

It supports many common job sites, but custom application forms may need manual fixes.

What is a good Simplify alternative?

Utterseen works in any browser and makes a resume tailored to each job before filling the application. Teal and Huntr cover resumes and tracking.
