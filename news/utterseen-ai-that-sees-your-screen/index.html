Utterseen is a voice AI that sees your screen, makes study guides and fills out job applications

November 19, 2026

In short: Utterseen is a voice AI assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. You hold a key, ask out loud about whatever you're looking at, and it answers out loud or does the task in that app: making study guides and flashcards, summarizing lectures, explaining homework, or making a tailored resume and filling out a job application. It's free to start.

How does Utterseen work?

Hold a key (Right Command on Mac, Right Ctrl on Windows), ask your question out loud, and let go. Utterseen takes one screenshot when you start talking, so it knows what you mean by "this," and answers out loud in plain words. It works on top of any app: a browser, a PDF, slides, a video, an email or a form.

What can students use it for?

Turning slides or notes into a study guide PDF or Anki flashcards, getting a homework problem explained step by step like a tutor, summarizing a lecture or YouTube video, and having it quiz you out loud on a chapter. It can also read a page out loud and explain it in simpler words, which helps with ADHD and dyslexia.

What does it do for job seekers?

Open a job posting and say "apply to this job." Utterseen makes a resume tailored to that job from the resume you added, keeping only the experience that fits, tells you how well you match, then fills out the application. For written questions it asks how you'd answer and writes it up. It stops before Submit so you can check everything.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is great for long conversations and writing from scratch, but you usually copy things into a chat window and copy the answer back. Utterseen skips that: it already sees what you're looking at and can act on it, like filling a form or pasting a reply, in the app you're using.

What about privacy?

It only listens while the key is held, takes one screenshot per question, and doesn't store recordings or screenshots. There's also a HIPAA option for nurses, caregivers and clinics.

Where can you get it?

Utterseen is available for Mac and Windows, including on the Microsoft Store. It's free to start, with a Pro plan for daily use.
