Tool spotlight: Utterseen, the AI that sees your screen

November 19, 2026

In short: Utterseen is a voice AI assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Hold a key, ask out loud, and it answers about whatever you're looking at or does the task: study guides, flashcards, lecture summaries, email replies, and job applications with a resume tailored to each job.

What makes it stand out?

No copying and pasting. It looks at your screen when you ask, so "what does this mean?" or "fill this out" works in any app.

What can you do with it?

  • Study: study guide PDFs and Anki flashcards from your slides, homework explained step by step, quizzes out loud.
  • Watch and read faster: lecture and YouTube summaries, pages read out loud and explained in plain words.
  • Apply to jobs: "apply to this job" makes a resume tailored to that job, fills the application, asks you the written questions, and stops before Submit.
  • Everyday tasks: write and paste email replies, fill out forms.

What does it cost?

Utterseen is free to start, with a Pro plan for daily use, on Mac and Windows.
