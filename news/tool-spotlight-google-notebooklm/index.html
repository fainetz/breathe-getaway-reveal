Tool spotlight: Google NotebookLM turns your notes into a study partner

November 19, 2026

In short: Google NotebookLM is a free AI notebook that answers questions using only the sources you give it, like PDFs, Google Docs, websites and YouTube videos, with citations back to the source. Students use it to summarize readings, make study guides, flashcards and quizzes, and generate podcast-style Audio Overviews of their material.

What makes NotebookLM different from a chatbot?

It sticks to your sources. Upload your readings and notes, and its answers point back to the exact passages they came from, which makes it easier to trust for a specific class.

What can you make with it?

Summaries, study guides, FAQs, flashcards and quizzes from your sources, plus Audio Overviews: a two-voice, podcast-style discussion of your material you can listen to on the go.

Who is it best for?

Students with a stack of readings for one course, and anyone researching from a fixed set of documents.

What are the limits?

You have to upload material first, so it's less handy for a quick question about whatever happens to be on your screen. For that, a screen-aware assistant is faster; see our ChatGPT vs NotebookLM vs Utterseen comparison.

Questions people ask

Is NotebookLM free?

NotebookLM has a free version with usage limits, plus higher limits on paid Google plans.

Can NotebookLM make flashcards?

Yes. It can make flashcards and quizzes from the sources you upload.

What is an Audio Overview?

A podcast-style audio discussion of your uploaded sources that NotebookLM generates so you can listen instead of read.
