How to fill out online job applications faster

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To fill out online job applications faster, keep your usual answers in one place, let a tool make a resume tailored to each job, and autofill the form instead of retyping your work history. Most of an application's time goes into retyping the same details, so that's where you save the most.

Why do online applications take so long?

Most company career sites make you create an account, upload a resume, then retype your work history, education and the same yes or no questions anyway, often across several pages. Do that for every job and it eats your whole day.

Does uploading your resume help?

Partly. Many sites try to prefill from your resume and get the basics right, but they often split jobs, mix up dates or skip sections. Check every entry before moving on.

What should you keep ready?

A document with your contact details, education, references and answers to common questions like work authorization, start date and salary expectations. Copying from one place beats retyping from memory every time.

How do you avoid a new password every time?

Use a password manager, or sign in with Google where the site allows it. Either way you can get back in later to check your application status.

Letting AI handle the form

Utterseen is an AI assistant that sees your screen. Open a job posting, hold a key and say "apply to this job," and it makes a resume tailored to that job, fills the application from it, and asks you the written questions one at a time before writing up your answers. It stops before Submit so you check everything first.

Questions people ask

Why do job sites make me create a new account every time?

Each employer runs its own career site, and accounts usually don't carry over between companies. A password manager or Google sign-in makes this much less painful.

Can AI fill out job applications for me?

Yes. Autofill tools can fill application forms from your resume. Utterseen also makes a resume tailored to each job first and leaves the final Submit to you.

How long should an online application take?

Often 20 to 45 minutes by hand because of the repeated work history sections. With saved answers and autofill, most of that becomes a few minutes of checking.
