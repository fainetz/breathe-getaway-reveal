Top 10 AI tools for students in 2026

November 18, 2026

Short answer: The top AI tools for students in 2026 are Utterseen (a voice AI that sees your screen and makes study guides and flashcards), ChatGPT and Claude (explanations and writing help), Google NotebookLM (summaries of your own notes), Quizlet and Anki (flashcards), Perplexity (research with sources), Otter (lecture transcripts), Grammarly (writing feedback) and Khanmigo (a tutor from Khan Academy).

1. What makes Utterseen different?

Utterseen is a voice AI for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Hold a key and ask about the slide, PDF, problem or video in front of you, and it answers out loud. It turns slides into study guide PDFs and Anki flashcards, explains homework step by step, summarizes lectures and quizzes you, without copying anything into a chat window.

2. What is ChatGPT best for?

General explanations, brainstorming and drafting. Ask it to explain a concept a different way or to check your reasoning. You paste in what you need help with.

3. What does NotebookLM do?

Google's NotebookLM lets you upload your own notes, PDFs and readings, then summarizes them and answers questions based only on those sources, which keeps answers tied to your class material.

4 and 5. Quizlet or Anki for flashcards?

Quizlet is easy to use and has millions of shared sets. Anki's spaced repetition shows hard cards more often, which is better for remembering long term. Both import cards made by AI.

6 to 10. What about the rest?

Claude handles long readings well. Perplexity answers research questions with links to sources. Otter records and transcribes lectures. Grammarly gives feedback on your writing. Khanmigo is Khan Academy's AI tutor that guides you instead of handing over answers.

How should you choose?

Pick by what slows you down. If it's copying things into chats and making study materials, start with Utterseen. For research, Perplexity. For memorizing, Anki. Most students only need two or three of these.

Questions people ask

What is the best AI tool for students?

It depends on the task. Utterseen is best for asking about anything on your screen and making study guides and flashcards by voice; ChatGPT for explanations; NotebookLM for your own notes; Anki for memorizing.

Is there a free AI that makes study guides?

Yes. Utterseen is free to start and makes study guide PDFs from whatever is on your screen. ChatGPT and NotebookLM can also draft study guides from material you give them.

What AI can make flashcards from my slides?

Utterseen makes Anki flashcards from slides or notes on your screen. You can also ask ChatGPT for question and answer pairs and import them into Anki or Quizlet.
